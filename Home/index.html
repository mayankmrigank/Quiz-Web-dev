Our Initiative

About the Scheme

Central Coalfields Limited (CCL) under its CSR initiative has been providing free coaching along with boarding and lodging to meritorious students since 2012 under CCL Ke Lal · CCL Ki Laadli.

📚

Free Coaching

Expert coaching for IIT-JEE, NEET and other prestigious engineering entrance examinations — completely free of charge.

🏠

Boarding & Lodging

Full accommodation and nutritious meals provided to all selected students throughout their preparation period.

🎓

IIT Alumni Faculty

Students are taught by CCL Officers who are IIT Alumni, bringing world-class teaching quality to every classroom.

🏆

Proven Results

Running successfully since 2012, the scheme has delivered tremendous results with 50+ IIT selections and counting.
